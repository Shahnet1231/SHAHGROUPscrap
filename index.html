کسٹمر کی معلوماتCustomer Information

Ye aapke apne business/customer ki details hain — inhi ke mutabiq Sale/Purchase slip aur PDF ka header banega.
Ye logo Sale/Purchase slip ke UPAR (header) mein aapke business ke naam ke sath dikhega.
Agar aapke paas apna designed letterhead (jaise RANA Metal Work wala) hai to yahan upload karein — ye Statement, Ledger aur Report ki PDF/Print mein poore page ke background ki tarah use hoga (upar wala Logo/Company Name is ki jagah nahi dikhega). Sirf JPG/PNG image chalegi — agar letterhead PDF format mein hai to pehle usay JPG/PNG mein export ya screenshot kar ke upload karein.
